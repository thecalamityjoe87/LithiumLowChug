Lithium LowChug

A guitar detuner by Isaac Joseph (Calamity Audio): play in standard and hear your guitar tuned lower (E♭, D, Drop C, B standard, an octave down). It works two ways:

  • As a plugin (Audio Unit, VST3 or LV2) on a guitar track in your DAW.
  • As a standalone app in Applications: plug in your guitar and play, no DAW needed.

Click Customize on the Installation Type page to choose which of these to install. All are installed by default.

The 18 factory presets are built in, so there's nothing else to download.
